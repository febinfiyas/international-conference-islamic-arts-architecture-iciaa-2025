02 / ABOUT US

A shared academic space

Where cultural heritage meets contemporary imagination.

The International Conference on Islamic Arts and Architecture brings together scholars, artists, architects and cultural practitioners for critical dialogue across disciplines, traditions and geographies.

The conference explores the enduring richness of Islamic artistic and architectural traditions—from calligraphy and geometric patterns to mosques, madrasas, historic cities and contemporary design. It encourages exchange, comparative perspectives and new approaches to understanding this living heritage.
